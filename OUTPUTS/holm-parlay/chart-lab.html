Six ways to look at the ticket

Chart options for the site, built from the recorded, graded tickets (Weeks 2 and 4–8). Pick the ones you want and I'll add them to the site. Week 3 was never placed, and Week 9's player props aren't graded in the records yet, so neither is plotted. The Standings stake vs. return chart stays as it is.
